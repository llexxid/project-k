<b>Sign-in and game progress</b>
Ludos processes game account information to sign you in, save progress and provide support. Google sign-in requests email, profile and authentication information. The game sends the authorization code provided by Google to Microsoft PlayFab to link your game account.

<b>Information associated with your account</b>
The game uses PlayFab and Azure to process your game account ID, stage, currencies, characters, equipment, skills and reward records. Your account also records the version of the terms you accepted and the time of acceptance for account and access management.

<b>Information on this device</b>
Your device keeps a copy of game state, tutorial progress, language, audio and display preferences, and records used during sign-in. The age you enter for alpha access is neither stored nor sent to the server. If you are ineligible, the device stores only the time when the age check will become available again.

<b>Ads and device notifications</b>
If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google Mobile Ads may process your IP address, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. Depending on service settings, PlayFab may process device information and app usage time.

You can change notification permission in Android settings at any time. You can play with notifications turned off. Accepting the game terms or allowing device notifications does not opt you into promotional messages.

<b>Support and deletion</b>
Players and their legal guardians can request access to, correction or deletion of account information, or restriction of processing. Use the account deletion guide below for instructions. Please do not send passwords or one-time codes to support.
